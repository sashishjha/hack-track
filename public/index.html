Strategy Guide

Avoid overcrowded arenasKaggle mega-competitions have 10,000+ teams. Same skill wins more on Zindi, AIcrowd, or Solafune with 100–500 participants.
Target sponsor sub-tracksIn large hackathons, don't fight for 1st overall. Pick one sponsor track and dominate it. These are judged separately with their own prize pool.
Your moat: cross-domain stackCombining embedded + robotics + CV + RL + edge AI is rare in hackathons. Projects at this intersection win sponsor tracks disproportionately.
AI tools = allowed (usually)Most modern hackathons expect you to use AI. Your edge is problem framing + domain knowledge, not writing boilerplate manually.

Why Bounties Beat Most Hackathons for Steady Income

No competition bracket — you either fix it or you don't. Paid per task. Multiple active tasks at once. No waiting for event dates. Your GitHub reputation compounds over time. Best for someone with strong Python, ML tooling, and robotics/CV open-source skills.
